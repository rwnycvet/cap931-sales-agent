**Likely Needs**  
- **Continuous, high‑sensitivity malware‑and‑ransomware detection** – MCU handles sensitive financial data for >630,000 members, making it a prime ransomware target.  
- **Fast, automated incident‑response capability** – New mobile app and Chelsea branch mean any endpoint breach must be contained quickly to keep services available.  
- **Centralized visibility and control across a heterogeneous endpoint fleet** – VaultRight investment and presence of a CTO indicate a growing, distributed set of devices needing unified oversight.  
- **Compliance‑driven continuous monitoring and logging** – With a CRO and regulated loan, mortgage, and credit‑card services, MCU must meet GLBA, NYDFS, and related audit‑ready reporting.  
- **Proactive threat‑hunting and threat‑intel integration** – High public profile and community partnerships increase exposure to sophisticated threat actors; proactive hunting helps uncover low‑and‑slow attacks.  

**Talking Points**  
- Position GreatShield EDR as “enterprise‑grade endpoint protection with audit‑ready compliance reporting, built for mid‑sized financial institutions without large security teams.”  
- Emphasize continuous monitoring on Windows, macOS, Linux workstations/servers to meet the need for high‑sensitivity malware detection.  
- Highlight one‑click device isolation and ransomware rollback to provide the fast, automated response MCU requires for mobile‑banking and branch continuity.  
- Note cloud‑managed console and centralized visibility to simplify oversight of MCU’s heterogeneous endpoint fleet.  
- Reference audit‑ready PDF reports mapped to NCUA/FFIEC guidance, aligning with the CRO’s compliance monitoring obligations.  
- Offer 24/7 managed alert triage so MCU’s limited security staff receives only confirmed threats, freeing internal resources.  
- Stress that GreatShield’s lightweight agent deploys through existing software tools, fitting MCU’s operational model and recent technology investments.
